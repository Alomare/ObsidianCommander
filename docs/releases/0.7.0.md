### Highlights

#### Added One Settings Dialog

The settings button on the war screen and **Edit** in the war list open the same dialog: the logo, name and description, then every rule of the war. **Create War** asks only for the name, the model and the preset.

#### Added Own Save Only and Allow Achievements

Two new settings for each war, both off by default. **Own Save Only** plays the war only from the save Obsidian made for it. **Allow Achievements** lets Steam achievements unlock while the war is played.

#### New Map Look

The war map shows only the sector numbers. Liberated sectors are light grey, front line sectors are bright yellow, and each faction has a home world sphere. War logos are square, with no plate behind them.

### Changes

- A new war is named Custom War unless given another name, and starts paused.
- Evolved wars are archived. An Evolved war file opens as a Vanilla war, without the Evolved data.
- The Enhanced War card in **Create War** shows Coming Later and can't be picked.
- The war list has no title, and each war shows the HELLDIVERS skull for the model.
- Obsidian can show **Import** and **Create War** in the Obsidian bar above the war list. Older Obsidian versions keep them on the list.
- When a war opens to LAN, the war screens tell Obsidian, so Obsidian on Linux can offer to open the LAN ports.

### Bug Fixes

- Fixed a black square at the top left of the war screens on Linux.
