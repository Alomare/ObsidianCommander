### Highlights

#### Added Open to Internet

Added an **Internet** switch beside **LAN** on the war screen. With it on, players outside the local network join the war by address, once the port shown is forwarded on the router. Wars open to LAN alone stay on the local network.

#### Signed Packages

Added a signature to every release. Obsidian 2.0 installs a downloaded package only when the signature is valid.

### Changes

- The LAN listener answers addresses of the local network and of VPN tools only, unless a war is open to the internet.
- A request that names a public website as the host is refused.
- A war keeps at most 5000 players.
