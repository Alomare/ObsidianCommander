### Highlights

#### Added Heroes of the Federation for Custom Wars

The war keeps the scores players upload after each mission, and the **Heroes of the Federation** tab lists them for every front and for the whole war. Before, opening the tab in a custom war closed the game.

#### Added the Proving Grounds Board

Proving Grounds results are kept for each squad: the best time, the influence and the playtime, listed for every front.

### Changes

- Removed the **Select** button from the war screen. The war list still has it, and **Play** in Obsidian picks the war.
- Buttons are no longer bold.

### Bug Fixes

- Fixed Proving Grounds trials of a custom war that started a mission of the wrong front or type.
